Full Price Is Cancelled: how to save before you spend

A practical guide to discounted gift cards, boosted offers, Perks Credit, stacking and the habits that make small savings add up.

Most student discount services ask you to look for a code at checkout. SUBTV Perks starts earlier: it lets you reduce the cost of the payment method itself.

What does “save before you spend” mean?

You purchase a gift card for less than its face value, then use the full gift card value with the participating retailer. A £100 gift card with a 10% saving costs £90, while still giving you £100 to spend.

How Perks works

1. Choose a brand

Browse brands relevant to a purchase you already intend to make.

2. Buy the gift card

Choose an amount, review the terms and pay securely through the app.

3. Activate and redeem

Follow the retailer-specific instructions for online or in-store redemption.

4. Look for more value

Check whether the gift card can be combined with sales, loyalty prices or promotional codes, and watch for boosted offers.

The habit that matters

The simplest behaviour is also the most valuable: check Perks before you spend. It turns savings from an occasional discovery into a repeatable part of the shopping journey.
