The core journey

Four steps from
browse to saving

Keep the explanation focused on the real transaction, then introduce rewards, voting and referrals as additional value.

01

Browse the latest offers

Search by brand, category or occasion. Each offer clearly shows the saving and where the gift card can be redeemed.

02

Choose an amount and pay

Select a gift card value and complete payment securely. Your purchase is fulfilled by Student Perks Ltd.

03

Activate when ready

Your gift card is stored in the Perks wallet until you need it. Activation reveals the redemption details.

04

Spend and earn

Use it with the retailer, collect relevant rewards and check back for the next boosted deal.
